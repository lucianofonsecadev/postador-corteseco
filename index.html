Postador Corte Seco

A private desktop tool that uploads and schedules videos to the YouTube channel CORTE SECO (@cortesecocuriosidadesecinema), used only by the channel owner.

What it does

  • Uploads the channel's own finished videos (Shorts and long-form) using the YouTube Data API v3 (videos.insert, resumable upload).
  • Sets the title, description, tags, language, "not made for kids" and the scheduled publish time chosen by the owner.
  • Sets the custom thumbnail (thumbnails.set) and adds the video to one of the channel's own playlists (playlistItems.insert).
  • Keeps a local log of what was uploaded (video ID and link), so the same video is never uploaded twice.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the CORTE SECO channel, on their own computer. It is not sold, not distributed and has no public sign-up. The owner signs in with their own Google account through Google's OAuth 2.0 consent screen and can revoke access at any time.
