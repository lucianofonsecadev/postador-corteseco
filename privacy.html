Privacy Policy

Postador Corte Seco · Last updated: November 8, 2026

"Postador Corte Seco" (the "Tool") is a private desktop application used only by the owner of the YouTube channel CORTE SECO to upload and schedule their own videos. This policy explains what data the Tool accesses, how it is used, stored and shared, and how to revoke access or delete it.

1. YouTube API Services

The Tool uses YouTube API Services. By using the Tool you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

2. What data the Tool accesses

After you sign in with your Google account and grant permission (OAuth 2.0, scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube), the Tool accesses only:

  • Your channel's basic information (channel title), to confirm the upload goes to the right channel.
  • The videos, thumbnails and playlists that the Tool itself creates or updates on your channel.

The Tool does not read comments, subscribers, analytics, messages, or any data from other users or channels.

3. How the data is used

Only to upload the video files chosen by the channel owner, set their title, description, tags, language, audience ("not made for kids"), scheduled publish time and thumbnail, and add them to the owner's playlists.

4. How the data is stored

  • The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer (file token.json). It is never sent to any server other than Google's.
  • A local log keeps the video ID, link, title and upload date of each video uploaded by the Tool, so the same video is not uploaded twice. Stored API data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days.
  • The Tool has no server, no database in the cloud, no analytics and uses no cookies.

5. Sharing

The Tool does not sell, share or disclose any user data to third parties. Data is exchanged only with Google/YouTube, as required to perform the upload.

6. Revoking access

You can revoke the Tool's access at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions (or myaccount.google.com/connections). After revoking, the Tool can no longer access your channel.

7. Deleting your data

Deleting the files token.json and postados.json on the computer removes all data stored by the Tool. You can also ask us to delete it by writing to the contact below; we will delete it within 7 days.

8. Children

The Tool is not directed to children and is used only by the adult owner of the channel.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

10. Contact
